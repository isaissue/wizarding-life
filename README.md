## English

A text-based life simulator set in the wizarding world. You aren't Harry, Hermione or anyone famous. You're one of the thousands of ordinary witches and wizards whose lives run alongside the story. Someone else's story might cross yours along the way.

### How it works

- **Random birth:** you might be born in the 1920s, during either wizarding war, or after the Battle of Hogwarts. Your family can be Muggle, half-blood or old pure-blood, rich or poor. Some lives start with a Squib.
- **Real statistics:** life expectancy, infant mortality and birth rates come from UK figures for your birth year, adjusted for wizards' longer lives.
- **Choices at key moments:** the Sorting, friendships, romance, careers, war, marriage, children and retirement each ask what you'll do. Your choices affect your stats, relationships, reputation and alignment.
- **Canon encounters:** you might share a boat with a young Hagrid, sit O.W.L.s near the Marauders, or meet Harry at the Ministry, depending on when and where you live.

### Features

- 🏰 **Hogwarts years:** Platform 9¾, the boats, the Sorting Hat, a weekly timetable, electives, yearly exams, O.W.L.s and N.E.W.T.s, House points, prefects and Head Boy/Girl
- 🎃 **School life:** clubs, Quidditch, Hogsmeade weekends (or sneaking in early), the Halloween feast, holidays that differ year to year, and the Forbidden Forest
- 📰 **Daily Prophet:** real wizarding-world headlines for every year you live through
- 👥 **Relationships:** parents, siblings, friends, rivals, dating, marriage and children, all with relationship values and birthdays
- 💼 **Careers:** Ministry roles, Auror, Healer, Hogwarts professor and many more, with promotions, workplace events and retirement
- ⚖️ **Values and alignment:** pure-blood and Muggle-born tensions, the Order of the Phoenix, and a good / neutral / evil scale that changes how people treat you
- 🎒 **Inventory and money:** Galleons, second-hand books, breakages, rare forest finds and gifts
- 🧙 **Pixel portrait:** a randomly generated 16×16 portrait that ages with you
- 🏆 **Achievements:** 49 achievements, collected across all your lives
- 🌏 **Bilingual:** switch between English and Traditional Chinese (Taiwan's official translation) at any time

### Running locally

It's a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step, dependencies or server. Download it and open it in a browser.

Achievements and past lives are saved in your browser's `localStorage`.

## 繁體中文

一款以魔法世界為背景的文字人生模擬遊戲。你不是哈利、妙麗，也不是任何主角，而是故事背後成千上萬平凡巫師中的一員。不過，說不定某一天，那些熟悉的身影會走進你的人生。

### 遊戲玩法

- **隨機出生**：你可能出生於 1920 年代、兩次巫師戰爭期間，或是霍格華茲大戰之後。家庭背景可能是麻瓜、混血或古老的純種家族，有富有貧。有些人生甚至以爆竹（沒有魔法的巫師後代）開局。
- **真實數據**：平均壽命、嬰兒死亡率與生育率均參考出生年份的英國統計資料，並按巫師較長的壽命調整。
- **人生抉擇**：分類、交友、戀愛、職業、戰爭、婚姻、子女與退休，每個重要關頭都由你決定。你的選擇會影響能力值、人際關係、聲望與陣營。
- **遇見原著角色**：視乎你生活的年代與地點，你可能和年輕的海格同坐一條船、在劫盜者身旁考普等巫測，或在魔法部遇見哈利。

### 遊戲特色

- 🏰 **霍格華茲歲月**：九又四分之三月台、乘船渡湖、分類帽、每週課表、選修課、期末考試、普等巫測（O.W.L.）與超勞巫測（N.E.W.T.）、學院分數、級長及男／女學生主席
- 🎃 **校園生活**：社團、魁地奇、活米村週末（或偷偷提早溜去）、萬聖節宴會、每年不同的假期活動，以及禁忌森林探險
- 📰 **預言家日報**：你經歷的每一年都有魔法世界的真實頭條新聞
- 👥 **人際關係**：父母、兄弟姊妹、朋友、對手、約會、婚姻與子女，每段關係都有好感度與生日
- 💼 **職業生涯**：魔法部職員、正氣師、治療師、霍格華茲教授等多種職業，包括升遷、職場事件與退休
- ⚖️ **價值觀與陣營**：純種與麻瓜出身的衝突、鳳凰會，以及會影響他人看待你的善良／中立／邪惡數值
- 🎒 **物品與金錢**：金加隆、二手課本、損壞賠償、禁忌森林的稀有收藏與禮物
- 🧙 **像素頭像**：隨機生成的 16×16 像素頭像，會隨年齡變化
- 🏆 **成就系統**：共 49 個成就，可跨越多次人生累積收集
- 🌏 **雙語介面**：隨時在英文與繁體中文（採用台灣官方譯名）之間切換

### 本機遊玩

整個遊戲只有一個獨立的 `index.html` 檔案，無需建置、套件或伺服器。下載後直接用瀏覽器開啟即可。

成就與過往人生紀錄會儲存在瀏覽器的 `localStorage` 中。
